Example 1. Graph \(y = x^2 + 3\) by completing the table of values and plotting the points.
🔗
Complete the table:
🔗
\(x\) \(y\) Ordered Pair
Solution.
Choose \(x\) values: \(-2, -1, 0, 1, 2\text{.}\)
🔗
\(x\) \(y = x^2 + 3\) Ordered Pair
\(-2\) \(7\) \((-2, 7)\)
\(-1\) \(4\) \((-1, 4)\)
\(0\) \(3\) \((0, 3)\)
\(1\) \(4\) \((1, 4)\)
\(2\) \(7\) \((2, 7)\)
Plot the points and connect them with a smooth curve to form a parabola opening upward with vertex at \((0, 3)\text{.}\)
